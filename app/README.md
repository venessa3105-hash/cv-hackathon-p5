# Fine-Grained Vehicle Recognition (CV Hackathon 2026, P5)

Upload a car photo to detect each vehicle and predict its make, model and year
(196 Stanford Cars classes). The app compares classical, deep learning and hybrid
pipelines and shows the intermediate image-processing steps.

Models are loaded from the Hugging Face Hub repo `vene-05/p5-convnext-cars`.
